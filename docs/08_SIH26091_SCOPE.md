# 08. SIH26091 Scope & Functional Boundaries

## Functional Architecture

1. **Enterprise Identity & Local Context**:
   - Village, district, state, pin-code tracking for hyper-local context.
   - Operating parameters (baseline monthly revenue/expense estimates).

2. **Core Financial Ledger Subsystem**:
   - Inflow/outflow transaction recording.
   - Receivables tracking with customer reliability scoring.
   - Payables tracking with supplier priority tiers.
   - Fixed operational expenses.

3. **Decision Engines (Deterministic Math)**:
   - Cashflow Engine (net cash aggregation).
   - Liquidity Engine (runway calculation & risk thresholds).
   - Financial Structure Engine (funding gap analysis).

4. **Future Advisory & Structuring (Phase 2+)**:
   - MoSJE scheme catalog matching.
   - Actionable business advisory recommendations.
