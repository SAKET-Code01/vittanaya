# 07. SIH26091 Context & Background

## Problem Statement Overview
- **Problem Code**: SIH26091
- **Domain**: Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment (MoSJE)
- **Title**: AI-Driven Hyper-Local Business Advisory and Financial Structuring Assistant for Rural Micro-Entrepreneurs

## Target Beneficiaries
1. **Rural Artisans & Crafts**: Pottery, handloom weaving, terracotta, woodcraft.
2. **Rural Agro-Processors**: Micro-milling, spice grinding, packaging collectives.
3. **Village Retailers & Service Units**: Local kirana, repair hubs, tailoring units.
4. **Self-Help Groups (SHGs)**: Women-led collective production units.

## Core Impact Goals
- Democratize financial intelligence and working-capital forecasting for informal entrepreneurs.
- Bridge the financial structuring gap to enable creditworthiness and government scheme access.
- Prevent severe cash crunches through proactive runway calculations.
